This edition follows Jake Handy's guide at [jakehandy.com/h1](https://jakehandy.com/h1) for **h1 v0.3.0** (4cff936, 20 November 2026). The previous edition, v1.0.0, documented **h1 v0.2.1** (5b697e5, 18 November 2026). Same words, same design; only the text that changed in h1 has changed here.

## What changed in h1 between v0.2.1 and v0.3.0

Two firmware releases came out in between: [v0.2.2](https://github.com/jakehandy/h1/releases/tag/v0.2.2) (19 November) and [v0.3.0](https://github.com/jakehandy/h1/releases/tag/v0.3.0) (20 November).

**New instruments and sounds**
- Two new drum instruments: **TILT** (electronic drum kits on a pinball table) and **PLUNK** (recorded hits on a steel tongue drum). h1 now has sixteen instruments. *(v0.3.0)*
- **LAVA** gets a ninth algorithm, **HOOVER**, the rave one. *(v0.3.0)*

**USB and MIDI**
- **USB audio**: the FM-1 shows up as an audio input, so you can record the whole mix straight into a computer or phone. *(v0.2.2)*
- **MIDI in with velocity**, over USB or the TRS jack. Notes from a MIDI keyboard are recorded and played back with their velocity, and steps keep it too. *(v0.2.2)*
- **MIDI out** over USB on channel 1: notes from the keys, arpeggiator and sequencer, and CC 20 to 23 as the knobs turn. *(v0.2.2)*
- **USB host**: hold OCT − and OCT + for a second to plug a USB-MIDI keyboard straight in, no computer needed. *(v0.3.0)*

**Sequencer and recording**
- **Loops are saved.** A loop and all its layers are saved as soon as a take closes and come back after a power-off. *(kept loop in v0.2.2; survives a power-off while playing from v0.3.0)*
- **LAYERS** is on by default, and each layer keeps its own instrument, algorithm, four knobs, ENV and LFO. The screen shows REC: NEW LAYER and LAYER 2, 3…, with up to sixteen layers. *(v0.2.2 and v0.3.0)*
- Loops hold **1,024 notes** and **BARS** goes up to **128**, with a **LOOP FULL** message when a note doesn't fit. *(v0.3.0)*

**Firmware fixes that don't change the guide text**
These are in Jake's v0.3.0 release notes but don't change any wording in the guide:
- ECHO no longer gets grainy the longer the FM-1 stays on.
- EDIT + MASTER no longer raises everything else's volume.
- BARD's drone holds and fades out instead of cutting off.

## What changed in this edition

- New pages for TILT and PLUNK, a new **USB audio and MIDI** section in the intro, and the source's USB line on the cover.
- Updated text in the reference, holds and combos (new OCT − + OCT + hold), switching between loop and step, loop and step settings, recording, writing steps and layers.
- **All fourteen FAQs** are now included (v1.0.0 had four), with answers copied word for word from the live page. "Is my loop saved when I power off?" now answers Yes.
- Cover grid is eight across; the contents page lists all sixteen instruments; 41 bookmarks.
- Unchanged and checked: the other fourteen instruments, all global settings pages, the metronome, the keyboard map and the screenshots.

## Not verified / known differences

- The site's pictures are animated. The existing fourteen artwork captures were kept (the v0.3.0 export caught different frames, one of them blank); TILT and PLUNK are new captures.
- TILT and PLUNK use the site's accent colours. BARD and KOI are a shade lighter on the site than in this edition, which keeps its existing colours.
- "handy-1" appears in the live page's text beside the wordmark but not in its printed export, so the cover is unchanged.
- PLUNK's 😎 is the one character drawn from a third font (a one-glyph subset of Noto Color Emoji, SIL OFL 1.1).
